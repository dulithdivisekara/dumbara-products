Born in the Dumbara Valley

Highland agricultural traditions preserved across generations in Wattegama, Kandy.

Nestled near the Knuckles mountain range, Dumbara has been celebrated for centuries as Sri Lanka's heartland for indigenous spices and artisanal Kithul palm tapping.

Dumbara Products was founded to preserve this authentic heritage while introducing modern hygienic packaging and laboratory purity standards. We work directly with hereditary tapping families and smallholder growers in Wattegama and Pathadumbara.
